The Meeting is the central object in Meetly. Every transcription, summary, and action item belongs to a meeting, and the entire workflow — from scheduling a call to reviewing your AI-generated notes — flows through it. Understanding how meetings are structured and how they progress through their lifecycle helps you get the most out of Meetly's automation.

## Meeting Lifecycle

A meeting moves through a predictable sequence of stages, from the moment you create it to the point where it's archived and ready for reference.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    Create a meeting via the dashboard or the API. Set the title, scheduled time, and invite participants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Meetly registers the meeting and, if configured, sends calendar invites to participants. The meeting status is set to `scheduled`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record / Upload">
    When the meeting starts, the Meetly bot joins your video call for live recording — or you upload an audio/video file manually. The status transitions to `in_progress`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transcribe">
    Meetly processes the audio and produces a timestamped, speaker-identified transcript. See [Transcription](/concepts/transcription) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Summarize">
    Once transcription completes, Meetly's AI generates a structured summary with key topics, decisions, and next steps. See [Summaries](/concepts/summaries) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Act">
    Review the transcript, summary, and auto-extracted action items in the dashboard or via the API. The meeting status moves to `completed`, and later to `archived`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meeting Statuses

As a meeting progresses through its lifecycle, its `status` field reflects the current stage. Use this field to drive your own workflows and integrations.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| `scheduled` | The meeting has been created and is waiting to start. No recording or transcription has begun. |
| `in_progress` | The meeting is currently being recorded or an uploaded file is actively being processed. |
| `completed` | Recording and processing are finished. The transcript, summary, and action items are available. |
| `archived` | The meeting has been moved to the archive, either manually or automatically after the retention period. It remains readable but no further processing will occur. |

## Participants

Add participants to a meeting at creation time or at any point afterwards via the API. Each participant is identified by a `name` and `email` address.

When a meeting reaches `completed` status, Meetly automatically emails each participant a copy of the summary if `email_notifications` is enabled on your account. You can override notification behavior per-meeting using the `notify_participants` flag on the meeting object.

To add a participant after a meeting has been created, send a `POST` request to `/v1/meetings/{meeting_id}/participants` with the participant's name and email. Participants can also be removed before the meeting starts using a `DELETE` request to the same endpoint.

<Note>
  Meetings are retained for **90 days** on the Free plan and **indefinitely** on Pro and Enterprise plans. Once a meeting is deleted, its transcript, summary, and action items are permanently removed.
</Note>
