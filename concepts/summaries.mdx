After transcription, Meetly generates a structured summary using AI, giving your team a clear record of what was discussed and decided. Summaries are linked to the meeting and transcript, and they're available via the dashboard and API as soon as generation completes.

## Summary Structure

Every summary Meetly generates follows a consistent structure so your team always knows where to find the information they need.

A summary always includes:

- **TL;DR** — A short paragraph capturing the essence of the meeting in plain language.
- **Key Topics** — A list of the main subjects discussed, each with a brief description.
- **Decisions Made** — A list of conclusions or resolutions the team reached.
- **Next Steps** — A list of follow-up actions and owners identified during the meeting.

## Customizing Summaries

You can tailor the style and focus of a summary by providing a custom system prompt when requesting generation. This lets you match the output to your audience — whether that's a formal executive brief or a quick, casual team update.

To generate a summary with a custom prompt, send a `POST` request to `/v1/summaries`:

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.meetly.ai/v1/summaries \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "meeting_id": "mtg_abc",
    "prompt": "Generate a formal executive summary focusing on decisions and risks."
  }'
```

If you omit the `prompt` field, Meetly uses its default summarization prompt, which aims for a balanced, neutral tone suitable for most teams.

## Regenerating Summaries

You can regenerate a summary at any time by sending another `POST` request to `/v1/summaries` with the same `meeting_id` and a new `prompt`. Meetly creates a new Summary object rather than overwriting the existing one, so all previous versions are preserved and accessible via `GET /v1/summaries?meeting_id={meeting_id}`.

This is useful when you need multiple formats of the same meeting — for example, a technical summary for the engineering team and a high-level briefing for leadership.

<Note>
  Summaries are generated within **60 seconds** of transcription completing on Pro and Enterprise plans. On the Free plan, summary generation may take up to 5 minutes depending on queue volume.
</Note>
