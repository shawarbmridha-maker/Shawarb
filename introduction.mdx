## What Meetly Does

Once you connect Meetly to your calendar or call it directly via the API, it handles the entire post-meeting workflow for you:

- **Transcription** — Meetly produces accurate, speaker-attributed transcripts in real time, supporting 30+ languages right out of the box.
- **Summarization** — After each meeting, Meetly uses LLM-powered analysis to generate a concise summary that captures the key decisions and discussion points.
- **Action item extraction** — Meetly automatically identifies and assigns action items from the conversation, so nothing falls through the cracks.
- **API & integrations** — A clean REST API lets you embed Meetly into your own products, push data to your CRM, sync with project management tools, or build custom workflows on top of meeting intelligence.
